02 Interaction

Move across the artwork to raise the colour. Touch and pen work by dragging. Heat sustain keeps feeding the goo after the pointer leaves; goo rise and goo dwell set how slowly the letters swell and settle back to crisp type. Goo dissolve decides whether heated letters merely swell or melt away completely. Words replaces the sweep with a capsule sized to one whole character, word or line at a time.

In pick mode, click a unit on the artwork to hold the effect on it and click again to release. Clear heat trail drops every pick.
